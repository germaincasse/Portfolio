Hopf

A 4D game engine and its editor, for worlds with four spatial dimensions.

An engine in the spirit of Unity or Godot, for games whose world has four spatial dimensions. The editor shows a 4D scene the only ways a screen can: as a slice through a hyperplane, or as a projection down to 3D.

What I built

All of it, in C++20 on OpenGL 4.6 and Dear ImGui. The 4D math, with rotations in six planes instead of three. The meshes of the regular polytopes and their cousins: tesseract, 16-cell, 24-cell, duoprisms, cubinder, spherinder. Slicing and projection, four display styles, and an editor with dockable panels, several viewports, picking, gizmos and a scene hierarchy.

On top of that, a first game layer: collisions and rigid bodies between hypersolids, script components, audio, and two small example games, a 4D minigolf and a 4D minesweeper. Math, geometry and scene code are covered by Catch2 unit tests.
