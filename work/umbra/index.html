Umbra

Every solar eclipse from 2000 BC to the year 3000, on a world map or a globe. No server, no build step, no dependencies: the shadow paths are computed on the fly, when the page needs them.

What I built

The astronomy engine: Sun and Moon positions from the Meeus series, Besselian elements derived from that geometry, ΔT after Espenak and Meeus, then the central line of each eclipse and the limits of its path. Click anywhere on the map and it computes the local circumstances at that point: type, magnitude, obscuration, duration.

Around it, the interface: two projections drawn on a canvas, the shadow animated as it sweeps across the Earth, a timeline, GeoJSON export of the computed paths, and French and English versions. On the modern era, the paths land within a few tens of kilometres of the NASA canon.
